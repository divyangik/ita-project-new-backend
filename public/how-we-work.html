How ITA Global works

Travel is the gateway. Opportunity is the destination.

We use curated travel as a gateway to discovering places to experience, live, invest, build relationships and create meaningful impact.

Schedule a discovery call →
Our approach

Five steps from first conversation to informed decision.

01

Understand your goals

Every engagement begins with a conversation.

Exploring a new destination, purchasing international real estate, relocating abroad, rewarding a high-performing team, creating a luxury journey, developing a hospitality concept or discovering an impact investment — understanding the objective lets us build the right experience around it.

02

Connect you with the right destination

Through our network of destination experts, developers and alliances.

Instead of simply showing you a destination, we help you understand it through carefully selected experiences, communities, properties, people and local perspectives.

03

Design the experience

A curated journey or strategic program around your objectives.

Accommodations, transportation, private experiences, property visits, community exploration, professional introductions, corporate programming, cultural experiences or investment site visits — every program creates context, access and deeper understanding.

04

Experience it firsthand

Important decisions should not be made from a brochure.

Meet trusted local professionals, explore communities, visit properties or projects, understand the culture and evaluate opportunities in their real-world environment. This is where travel becomes discovery.

05

Move forward with confidence

The journey does not end when the trip ends.

When you identify an opportunity, we facilitate introductions to the right local professionals and partners — whether the outcome is another journey, a purchase, relocation, a corporate partnership, a hospitality project or an impact initiative.
