02 / Day by day

Ten days, one graceful rhythm.

Designed for travelers who value cultural depth, seamless flow and a more graceful rhythm throughout the journey.

01Arrival02Spiritual Tokyo03Culinary Tokyo04Hakone05Osaka06Nara07Kyoto08Tea & Evening09Immersion10Departure
  1. Day 01

    Tokyo

    Arrival in Tokyo

    • Arrival in Japan and private transfer to your hotel.
    • The remainder of the day is left at leisure to settle in and begin taking in the rhythm of the city at your own pace.
  2. Day 02

    Tokyo

    Spiritual centers & cultural discovery

    • Begin with one of Tokyo’s most revered temple precincts.
    • Continue into the city’s cultural heart through museums, shrines and a meaningful introduction to Japanese traditions.
    • The day concludes with a welcome dinner, setting the tone for the journey ahead.
    BreakfastWelcome dinner
  3. Day 03

    Tokyo

    Culinary immersion & personal interests

    • Explore Tokyo through its food culture and contemporary character.
    • Time is dedicated to market discovery and culinary experiences.
    • A more personalized afternoon may focus on art, sake or traditional martial heritage.
    Breakfast
  4. Day 04

    Tokyo → Hakone

    Into the mountains of Hakone

    • Depart Tokyo for Hakone, where the atmosphere shifts toward stillness, scenic beauty and restorative calm.
    • Surrounded by the landscapes of Fuji-Hakone-Izu National Park, this chapter includes art and mountain views.
    • Overnight stay in a distinguished ryokan.
    Breakfast
  5. Day 05

    Hakone → Osaka

    Bullet train to Osaka

    • After breakfast, continue onward by bullet train to Osaka.
    • Long celebrated for its vibrant food culture, the city introduces a more energetic rhythm.
    • The evening centers on culinary discovery and the lively spirit of its renowned dining districts.
    Breakfast
  6. Day 06

    Osaka · Nara

    Nara & ceremonial culture

    • Spend the day in Nara, where Japan’s spiritual and historical foundations come into clearer focus.
    • Visit ancient temples and experience shrine traditions and enduring ceremonial culture.
    • Return to Osaka in the evening.
    Breakfast
  7. Day 07

    Osaka → Kyoto

    Arrival in Kyoto

    • Transfer to Kyoto and begin exploring the city’s iconic cultural landscape.
    • Temple architecture, contemplative gardens and the quiet beauty of historic districts that have shaped the image of timeless Japan.
    Breakfast
  8. Day 08

    Kyoto

    Tea traditions & evening elegance

    • A day devoted to Kyoto’s refined rituals and living traditions.
    • Expect a gentler pace, with moments centered on ceremony, hospitality and the distinctive grace that defines the city’s cultural identity.
    Breakfast
  9. Day 09

    Kyoto

    Extended immersion & personalization

    • The additional day in Kyoto allows a more layered and personalized conclusion to the journey.
    • Use it for deeper cultural exploration, artisan encounters, private guiding or additional culinary experiences.
    • Or simply enjoy the city with greater ease and intention.
    Breakfast
  10. Day 10

    Kyoto → Departure

    Depart Kyoto

    • After breakfast, private transfer for onward departure.
    • The journey concludes with a final sense of calm and completion, having experienced Japan through a balance of beauty, precision and cultural depth.
    Breakfast
