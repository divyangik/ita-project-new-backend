01 / The program

Not a sightseeing vacation. A place to decide.

Boquete is a highland town in western Panama, known for its spring-like climate, coffee estates and an established international community. This program is designed for prospective residents, retirees, second-home buyers and investors who want to see the market for themselves before they commit.

Over three days of personalized real estate touring, ITA Global introduces you to the neighborhoods, the property types and the people who make relocation work, from realtors and attorneys to residency specialists and healthcare providers. It is, in ITA Global's words, “not a traditional sightseeing vacation.”

02 / Day by day

Five days, one clear decision.

Every touring day is shaped around your goals, property type and budget, agreed at orientation on arrival.

01Arrival02Discovery I03Relocation04Discovery III05Departure
  1. Day 01

    Tocumen Airport → Panama City → David → Boquete

    Arrival in the highlands

    • International flights must land before 12:00 PM to connect with the afternoon domestic flight to David.
    • Your private driver meets you at Enrique Malek International Airport for the drive up to Boquete.
    • Check in at Lucero Golf & Country Club.
    • Orientation: your goals, property type, budget and touring strategy.
    Welcome dinner
  2. Day 02

    Boquete

    Real Estate Discovery I

    • Market overview tour of Central Boquete, Alto Boquete, Jaramillo, El Salto, Los Naranjos, Volcancito, Palmira and Lucero.
    • Compare elevation, climate, rainfall, road access, walkability, healthcare, pricing and rental potential.
    • Personalized property appointments, with lunch at a local restaurant.
    • Evening at leisure.
    BreakfastLunch
  3. Day 03

    Boquete · Lucero

    Real Estate II, Relocation & the Lucero Lifestyle

    • Morning of focused property touring.
    • Meetings with real estate and legal professionals: title checks, due diligence, closing, corporations and foundations, and residency.
    • Healthcare overview, including hospitals in nearby David.
    • An informal meeting with a local expat.
    • After lunch, a round of golf or a massage at Lucero, then a free evening.

    Tee times and massage appointments are subject to availability.

    BreakfastLunchDinner on your own
  4. Day 04

    Boquete → David → Panama City

    Real Estate Discovery III

    • Check out of Lucero.
    • Final touring to narrow your search: revisit favorites, see new listings, meet developers and review rental potential and ownership costs.
    • Lunch with your real estate professional to discuss next steps. “There is no obligation to purchase.”
    • Flight from David to Panama City and a farewell dinner.
    BreakfastLunchFarewell dinner
  5. Day 05

    Panama City → Tocumen Airport

    Panama City & departure

    • Breakfast and a free morning in the city.
    • Private transfer to Tocumen International Airport.
    • ITA Global can keep coordinating follow-up conversations after you return home.
    Breakfast
